## 1. Google Antigravity

- **Antigravity App `v2.33.0` — Business `/swarm`, 200 MB Media & Faster PDFs (Nov 17):** Antigravity opened `/swarm` multi-agent teams and UI plugin panels to Business and Enterprise accounts, raised side-panel audio/video playback from 20 MB to 200 MB (plus `.amr` voice attachments), added in-page `Cmd/Ctrl+F` browser search, and sped up dense PDF scrolling. For a PO, you can now review full stakeholder call recordings, navigate large RFP or spec PDFs smoothly, and check live GitHub PR status pills in Overview without opening code. [Antigravity Changelog](https://antigravity.google/docs/changelog/)
- **Legacy Model Removals Effective Today (Nov 18):** Google Antigravity is removing `Claude Sonnet 4.6 (thinking)`, `Claude Opus 4.6 (thinking)`, and `GPT-OSS-120b` from its model selector on November 18, 2026. Update any team guides or saved routines that reference those older options to `Gemini 4 Hydrogen`, `Orchestrator`, or `Claude Sonnet/Opus 5.5`. [Antigravity Models](https://antigravity.google/docs/models/)

## 2. Claude (Anthropic)

- **Claude Desktop Release Channels (`Stable` vs. `Latest`, Nov 17):** Mac and Windows users on Claude Desktop `v4.29.0+` can now choose between `Stable` (default, tested longer) and `Latest` (updated roughly daily) under `Settings > Desktop app`, while IT-managed devices stay locked to `Stable`. For a PO, switching your personal machine to `Latest` lets you try new non-code features sooner while keeping team workstations predictable. [Claude Release Notes](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12138966-release-notes) · [Install Claude Desktop](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/10065433-install-claude-desktop)

## 3. Google Gemini & Google Workspace

- **Gemini Tasks Goes Free in Six Countries with Gemini 4 Hydrogen (Nov 17):** Google began rolling out Gemini's `Task` mode for free across the U.S., Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India powered by Gemini 4 Hydrogen, alongside U.S. previews for calling businesses, checking utility portals via Password Manager, and paying with Google Pay under user spending caps. For a PO, this signals Google's push toward multi-step background assistants (`Task` toggle and forwarding emails to `gemini@google.com`) worth tracking as similar automation reaches Workspace. [Google Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/tasks-in-gemini-free-new-features/)
- **Gemini-Powered Data Classification in Google Drive (GA, Nov 17 / Nov 18 Cairo):** Google Workspace admins can now classify Drive files using plain-English Gemini instructions—with a preview mode to test rules before rollout and real-time label reporting—instead of manually tagging training files. This helps protect sensitive PRDs, school contracts, and discovery notes from accidental sharing or unauthorized AI agent access without manual labeling overhead. [Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/gemini-powered-data-classification-in-Google-Drive-is-now-generally-available.html)
- **Time Zone Abbreviations in Google Calendar on Web (Nov 17):** Google Calendar on the web now supports searching and selecting common time zone abbreviations (such as `EST`, `PST`, or `GMT`) directly inside event and secondary time zone pickers. This speeds up scheduling calls across distributed engineering teams, international schools, and external partners. [Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/schedule-more-easily-across-time-zones-with-time-zone-abbreviations-in-Google-Calendar-on-the-web.html)

## 4. ChatGPT / OpenAI (Lowest Priority)

- **GPT-6.2 Astra & GPT-6.2 Sol Launch (Nov 17):** OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra (aimed at complex multi-step work, documents, spreadsheets, and slides with better template adherence) alongside GPT-6.2 Sol for faster everyday tasks across Plus, Pro, Enterprise, and Edu plans in ChatGPT Work. For a PO, stronger layout and template adherence reduces manual cleanup when drafting executive decks or KPI spreadsheets. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol/)
- **ChatGPT for Sales & 30+ Partner Plugins (Nov 17):** OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Sales, combining a first-party Sales plugin with 30+ partner integrations (including Salesforce, HubSpot, Gong, Granola, and Docusign) and background `dots` agents for account research, meeting prep, and pipeline reviews on Business and Enterprise plans. POs and GTM teams can use the expanded Gong, Granola, and HubSpot connectors to pull customer objections and call insights straight into roadmap prioritization. [OpenAI Sales](https://openai.com/business/solutions/sales/) · [OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001707-getting-started-with-chatgpt-for-sales)
- **Claude Fable 5.2 vs. GPT-6 Astra Benchmark (Nov 18):** Sid Saladi published a blind 16-task comparison across Claude Fable 5.2, GPT-6 Astra, Gemini 4 Carbon, GLM-5.3, and Qwen3.8-Max measuring scores, cost, and hallucination rates, plus a copyable Model Router pack. It gives POs a practical reference for deciding which model to pick for research synthesis, PRDs, or structured builds. [Sid Saladi](https://sidsaladi.substack.com/p/claude-fable-5-2-vs-gpt-6-astra)
