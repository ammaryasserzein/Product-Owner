## 1. Google Gemini & Gemini Notebook

- **Shareable Skills & Drive/Notebook/Photos Files in Gemini App (Nov 16):** You can now share custom Gemini Skills with View or Edit permissions and attach live-updating Google Drive files, Gemini Notebooks, or Google Photos (up to 100 MB per skill). For a PO, this lets your product team share one standardized PRD or stakeholder-comms skill grounded in a live Drive doc or research notebook. [Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/share-skills-add-files-from-Drive-Notebook-Photos-to-skills-Gemini-app.html)
- **Interactive Sites in Gemini Notebook (Nov 16):** Typing `"make me a site"` in Gemini Notebook chat turns your uploaded notes and sources into shareable interactive dashboards, timelines, or project trackers you can open in a new tab. For a PO, you can convert discovery interviews or sprint notes into an interactive prototype or stakeholder tracker without writing code. [Google Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/interactive-sites/)
- **Plain-Language Automations via Gemini in Apps Script (GA, Nov 16):** The Gemini side panel inside `Extensions > Apps Script` is now generally available, letting non-coders describe workflows across Gmail, Sheets, Docs, and Calendar in plain English instead of writing JavaScript. This helps a PO automate routine status sheets or inbox-to-calendar triage without engineering support. [Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/customize-and-extend-workspace-with-apps-script.html)
- **Compare `.docx` & Google Docs Without Converting (Nov 16):** Google Docs' **Compare documents** tool now supports Microsoft Word (`.docx`) files directly without converting them first. When external schools or stakeholders send Word redlines, you can diff changes against your Google Doc PRD without creating duplicate Drive files. [Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/review-edits-across-microsoft-word-docx-and-Google-Docs-files.html)
- **Separate Gemini History & AI Training Controls (Nov 16):** Google split Gemini's single "Keep Activity" toggle into separate **History** and **Generative AI Model Training** switches in the Privacy Hub. You can now keep a searchable history of product brainstorming chats while opting out of having your prompts and uploaded files used for model training. [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/16/gemini-history-ai-training/)
- **Google Pics Upgrades to Nano Banana 2.1 & 66 Regional Voices (Nov 16):** Google Pics inside Docs and Slides now runs on Nano Banana 2.1 for cleaner text rendering and more accurate image edits, while Gemini Live and Listen gained 66 new regional voices on Android and iOS. [Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/google-pics-upgrades-to-nano-banana-21-for-higher-quality-image-generation-and-editing.html) · [Google Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/regional-voices/)
- **Google Classroom Audit Log Details (Nov 16 — EdTech Radar):** Google Classroom admin audit logs now record the **Actor application name** and **User device ID** alongside affected class resources. This is a practical EdTech security benchmark for Schoolvoice when school admins need to trace whether a change came from a connected app or a specific device. [Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/11/more-detail-google-classroom-log-events-admin.html)

## 2. Obsidian (`obsidian-skills`)

- **`obsidianmd/obsidian-skills` `v1.2.0` (Nov 16):** Added an official Gemini CLI extension, a new skill for `ob` (Obsidian Headless CLI) to sync and publish vaults with `--dry-run` safety, and Bases support for kanban views and `groupOrder`. Updating keeps your AI assistants compatible with Gemini tools and accurate when editing `.base` kanban boards. [GitHub v1.2.0](https://github.com/obsidianmd/obsidian-skills/releases/tag/1.2.0)

## 3. ChatGPT / OpenAI (Lowest Priority)

- **Custom GPTs Retiring Dec 11 — Migrate to Portable Skills (Nov 17 Guide):** OpenAI is retiring Custom GPTs on December 11, 2026 (Feb 11, 2027 for qualifying Enterprise and FedRAMP workspaces) in favor of Plugins and Skills; the built-in migration button keeps instructions and knowledge files but drops custom actions and model choices. Sid Saladi's PO/PM guide walks through moving Custom GPTs into open `SKILL.md` folders that run in both ChatGPT and Claude before the cutoff. [OpenAI Help FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001519-custom-gpt-retirement-and-migration-faq) · [Sid Saladi](https://sidsaladi.substack.com/p/your-custom-gpts-stop-working-dec)
