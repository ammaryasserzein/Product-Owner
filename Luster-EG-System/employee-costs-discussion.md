# Future HR & Payroll Module Discussion

Currently, the system is designed to handle employee costs as a single lump-sum expense (e.g., "Salaries & Bonuses") entered by the accountant to keep the MVP lean.

However, there is a future requirement to build a dedicated module for tracking individual employee costs. When you are ready to discuss this in a new conversation, we can drill into the following:

- How to structure individual employee profiles.
- Tracking promotions, bonuses, and deductions per employee.
- Permissions around who can view individual salaries vs. the aggregate lump sum.
- How this granular data feeds back into the main company expense calculations.
