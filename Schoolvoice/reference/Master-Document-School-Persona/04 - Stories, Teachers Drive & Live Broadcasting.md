# 04 — Stories, Teachers Drive & Live Broadcasting

> [!abstract] Navigation
> **Hub**: [[_Master Document Hub]] | **Mental Model**: [[00 - How Schoolvoice Works (Product Mental Model)]] | **Prev**: [[03 - Chat & Voice Calls]] | **Next**: [[05 - Reports, District Management & Admin TBAs]]

> [!tip] Product Lens — Why These 3 Engagement Modules Are Critical
> Actionable Messages fail if parents only open a school app once a month. **Stories**, **Teachers Drive**, and **Schoolvoice Live** give parents a warm, useful reason to open the app every day—directly lifting Actionable Message read and completion rates.

These three modules drive **daily parent engagement**, **educational resource sharing**, and **real-time event participation** within a private, school-controlled environment.

---

## Side-by-Side Comparison

| Dimension | Stories | Teachers Drive | Video Live Broadcasting (`Schoolvoice Live`) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Core Purpose** | Share visual daily school/class moments privately (Instagram-style) | Centralized cloud file repository on each teacher's profile | Secure real-time live video events, Q&As, and reviews |
| **Audience / Privacy** | Strictly authorized parents; school-wide or targeted groups | `Public` (all parents/students) or `Private` (specific individuals/groups) | Whole school community or class-specific activity |
| **Permissions** | Teachers can post **only** for their own students' parents | Managed per folder by each teacher | Admins grant broadcast permission per teacher |
| **Interactivity** | Likes, Local Saves *(also lists `Views:` with no description)* | Browse & download directly by subject/topic/class folder | Live viewer Q&A and real-time interaction |
| **Safety & Access** | **AI content moderation**, parent reporting, admin filter dashboard | Direct access from teacher's Schoolvoice profile | In-app push notification to tune in—no external links/invites |

## Module Breakdowns

### 1. Stories
- **Engagement Loop**: Connects parents visually to music classes, sports events, and science experiments, sparking richer home conversations beyond *"How was your day?"*.
- **Interactive Metrics**:
  - **Likes**: Expresses parent appreciation and motivates teacher posting.
  - **Local Saves**: Lets parents download/keep cherished moments locally.
  - **Views**: *(Bullet present in source doc without description)*.
- **Governance**: Automated **AI moderation** ensures appropriateness; parents can **report** concerning stories; admins monitor everything via a filterable dashboard with alert notifications.

### 2. Teachers Drive
- **Unified Repository**: Replaces scattered emails and WhatsApp files with structured folders (by subject, topic, or class) for assignments, weekly plans, homework, and study materials.
- **Granular Privacy**: Folder-level `Public` vs `Private` permissions let teachers share class-wide handouts or targeted student resources in a few clicks.

### 3. Video Live Broadcasting (Schoolvoice Live)
- **Zero-Friction Tuning In**: School administration or permitted teachers start a broadcast, and targeted parents receive an automatic **in-app notification**—eliminating Zoom/Meet invite links.
- **Primary Use Cases**: School celebrations, special events, interactive parent Q&A sessions, extra class activities, and pre-exam reviews.
